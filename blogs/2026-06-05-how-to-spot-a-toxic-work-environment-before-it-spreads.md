This article identifies seven warning signs of toxic work environments, including high turnover, fear of speaking up, disrespectful behavior, poor communication, lack of employee support, excessive competition, and struggling managers. Toxic cultures develop gradually and can be addressed through listening to employees, investing in manager support, and prioritizing mental health resources. The piece emphasizes reinforcing recognition and fairness while redesigning work practices to create healthier environments.
