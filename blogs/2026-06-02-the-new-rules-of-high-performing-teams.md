High-performing teams succeed through healthy dynamics rather than individual talent alone, according to this Lyra Health article. Managers need proper support, and sustainable performance relies on strong communication, clear priorities, and team recovery periods. The piece argues that organizations must shift focus from individual output to team well-being as the foundation of lasting high performance.
